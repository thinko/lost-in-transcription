A Chrome / Edge extension that translates Microsoft Teams® live closed captions as they appear. You choose the translation backend (LibreTranslate, Google Cloud, DeepL, or OpenAI-compatible APIs — including local LLMs).

Why use this?

Teams offers caption translation, but it can be limited in languages, may stop when a meeting is recorded, and often misses product and technical terms. Lost in Transcription runs alongside Teams captions, uses your backend, and includes a user-editable glossary to fix recurring transcription mistakes.
