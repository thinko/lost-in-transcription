Typical meeting flow

  1. Join a Teams meeting in the browser on a supported host.
  2. Turn on Live captions in Teams.
  3. Ensure the extension is enabled (toolbar icon).
  4. Translations appear inline or in the side panel, depending on Display settings.

Keyboard shortcuts

Shortcut Action
Alt+Shift+T Toggle inline vs side panel display
Alt+Shift+E Export transcript (format from popup defaults)
Ctrl+click (word) Open glossary popover on that word (captions or translation)

On macOS, use Ctrl+click as configured; the popover targets the word under the cursor.

After an extension update

If translation stops after reloading the extension, use Restart translation on the Dashboard (or reload the Teams tab) so content scripts reconnect.
